Use cases

What the OS lets you do.

  • Meeting summaries

    Summarize a call while Luca keeps running quietly on your desktop.

  • Switch models mid-task

    Move from Luca Prime to a local model without losing your context.

  • Review before it sends

    Luca drafts a file or a message; you approve it before anything leaves.

  • Hands-free wake word

    Keep working while Luca listens for "Hey Luca" in the background.

  • A visible presence

    Bring up the Hologram for a visual presence during a call or demo.

  • Bring your own key

    Route a sensitive task through your own API key instead of the default.

  • Inspectable memory

    See exactly what Luca remembers, and clear anything you don't want kept.

  • A full audit trail

    Review every action Luca has taken. Nothing runs unlogged in the background.

The idea

What an AI operating system should be.

LucaOS is our take on an operating system for AI — one that lives on the device, stays under your control, and moves with you from screen to screen.

  1. 01

    Native to the device

    A host-native layer that becomes part of the device it runs on — desktop today, more surfaces over time.

  2. 02

    Always under your control

    Every real action is shown to you and waits for your approval before it runs.

  3. 03

    Yours to route

    Send work to Luca Prime, a local model, or your own key. Your memory stays the same on every device.

  4. 04

    Nothing hidden

    See what Luca remembers, clear what you don't want, and review everything it has done.

Start with LucaOS on your desktop.

The OS begins on the desktop — with Memory & Continuity, VoiceHUD, and Model Router built in — and grows across your devices from there. Prefer the browser first? The web app needs nothing to install.
