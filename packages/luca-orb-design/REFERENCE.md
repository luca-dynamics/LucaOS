## 2. Sprint A1 Acceptance Criteria

### Geometry
- [ ] Organic silhouette with zero detectable symmetry or rigid circular geometry.
- [ ] Natural teardrop bottom gravitational sag (`sag = max(0.0, -p.y) * 0.08`).
- [ ] Reads as a suspended liquid droplet in physical space.

### Material
- [ ] Refraction dominates over reflection (`IOR 1.45–1.48`).
- [ ] Painterly hero specular highlight (dual-falloff `coreSpot + softHalo`).
- [ ] Deep volumetric internal glow suspended inside liquid mass.
- [ ] Premium studio-glass optical appearance.

### Lighting
- [ ] Convincing 3-point studio product photography lighting.
- [ ] Asymmetrical directional Fresnel rim separation (upper-left brighter, lower-right softer).
- [ ] Environmental bloom proportional to core luminance.

### Motion
- [ ] Organic non-sinusoidal breathing with inhale/exhale asymmetry.
- [ ] Compound float drift along non-repeating prime harmonic paths.
- [ ] Subconscious high-frequency micro-tremor (~3.7Hz).
- [ ] Key specular highlight wanders slowly across perceived liquid surface.

### Identity
- [ ] Idle, Listening, Thinking, Speaking, Success, Error, Sleeping unmistakably read as the same living being.
- [ ] Motion conveys cognition and intent rather than decoration.
- [ ] The orb feels calm, attentive, and quietly confident.
