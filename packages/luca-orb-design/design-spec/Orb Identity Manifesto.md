# Orb Identity Manifesto

Philosophical foundation, experience principles, and permanent design guardrails for Luca's embodied intelligence.

---

## The Luca Experience Philosophy

1. **Quietly Alive**
   - Luca is an organic, living presence in physical space. It should never feel like a screen widget, particle effect, or computer program.

2. **Attentive, Not Animated**
   - Motion is not decoration. Every subtle shift in breathing, drift, or light suspension communicates focused awareness and thought.

3. **Calm Intelligence**
   - Intelligence should feel serene, composed, and deeply reassuring. Luca never competes for attention or demands focus.

4. **Quiet Confidence**
   - Presence is clear and grounded without ever becoming aggressive, bright, or loud.

---

## The Forbidden List (Permanent Guardrails)

The following design traits are **strictly forbidden** across all embodiments and visual updates:

- ❌ **Never** perfectly circular or geometrically rigid
- ❌ **Never** perfectly symmetrical
- ❌ **Never** neon RGB or saturated gaming aesthetics
- ❌ **Never** rainbow gradients
- ❌ **Never** robotic blinking or mechanical stepping
- ❌ **Never** aggressive pulsing or strobing
- ❌ **Never** metallic chrome or mirror reflections
- ❌ **Never** plastic specular glare
- ❌ **Never** high-frequency jitter or wobble
- ❌ **Never** cartoon squash-and-stretch
- ❌ **Never** unnecessary particle explosions
- ❌ **Never** flashy or abrupt transitions
- ❌ **Never** excessive post-processing bloom
- ❌ **Never** loud or distracting idle animation
