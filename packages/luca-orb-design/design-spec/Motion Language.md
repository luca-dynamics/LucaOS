- **Deep Breath Modulation**: Audio energy deepens the inhalation scale.

### 2. Float Drift
- **Compound Frequencies**: Motion combines two irrational prime harmonic frequencies so floating path never repeats.
- **Lateral Offset**: Includes subtle horizontal drift alongside vertical rise and fall.

### 3. Micro-Tremor
- **Subconscious Frequency**: High-frequency surface tension tremor (~3.7Hz).
- **Subtle Amplitude**: Barely perceptible when stared at directly; only felt as liquid vitality.
