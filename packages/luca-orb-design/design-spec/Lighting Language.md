# Lighting Language

Specification for studio product photography lighting setup for Luca's embodiments.

---

## 3-Point Studio Rig

1. **Key Light (Broad & Soft)**
   - Positioned in the upper-left quadrant (`[-0.4, 0.6]`).
   - Broad painterly falloff simulating a large softbox light source.

2. **Rim Light (Separation)**
   - Asymmetrical Fresnel rim lighting.
   - Highlights the silhouette on the upper-left, fading gracefully into shadow on the lower-right.

3. **Fill Light (Shadow Softener)**
   - Positioned lower-right (`[0.5, -0.4]`).
   - Prevents pitch-black dead shadows inside the glass volume.

4. **Environmental Bloom**
   - Belongs to the atmosphere surrounding the orb, not post-processing noise.
