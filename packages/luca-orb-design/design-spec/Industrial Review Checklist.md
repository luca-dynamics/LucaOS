# Industrial Review Checklist

The formal non-engineering industrial design review rubric for Luca's embodiments.

---

## Visual & Physical Critique Rubric

### 1. Anatomy & Geometry
- [ ] **Silhouette**: Does the shape immediately read as a living liquid form rather than a geometric sphere?
- [ ] **Centroid & Sag**: Is the teardrop bottom gravitational sag natural and balanced?
- [ ] **Asymmetry**: Is asymmetry organic without looking broken or deformed?
- [ ] **Suspension**: Does the body feel weightlessly suspended in physical space?

### 2. Glass Authenticity & Optics
- [ ] **Material Realism**: Does the outer shell read as thick optical glass rather than clear plastic?
- [ ] **Refraction Dominance**: Does background light bend and refract convincingly through the volume?
- [ ] **Subsurface Cloudiness**: Is interior clarity balanced between transparency and liquid density?

### 3. Core Light & Subsurface Glow
- [ ] **Depth**: Does internal luminance feel suspended deep inside the liquid mass rather than painted on top?
- [ ] **Core Positioning**: Is the inner core centered slightly off-axis to impart an organic "looking up" quality?
- [ ] **Corona Falloff**: Does inner light fade smoothly into the surrounding glass without harsh steps?

### 4. Painterly Specular Highlights
- [ ] **Hero Highlight**: Is the primary highlight a painterly, soft, floating light source embedded in the glass?
- [ ] **Specular Softness**: Are hard circular computer-graphics specular dots completely eliminated?
- [ ] **Light Source Alignment**: Does the highlight vector match studio product photography axes?

### 5. Edge Reflectivity & Rim Lighting
- [ ] **Asymmetrical Fresnel**: Is the rim brighter along the upper-left key axis and softer along the lower-right shadow edge?
- [ ] **Natural Separation**: Does the rim cleanly separate the orb from dark and light backgrounds without a uniform halo?

### 6. Environmental Grounding (Bloom & Ripples)
- [ ] **Bloom Subtlety**: Is ambient bloom soft and proportional to internal core brightness?
- [ ] **Ripple Subtlety**: Are environmental ripples barely perceptible cues that never distract from the body?

### 7. Shadow & Physical Weight
- [ ] **Contact Shadow**: Does the elliptical shadow beneath the orb ground it in space without feeling heavy?
- [ ] **Float Lift**: Does the shadow contract and soften naturally as the orb drifts upward?

### 8. Living Motion Character
- [ ] **Asymmetric Breathing**: Is the inhale/exhale rhythm non-sinusoidal and organic?
- [ ] **Lateral Float Drift**: Does floating follow non-repeating compound harmonic paths?
- [ ] **Subconscious Tremor**: Is high-frequency surface tremor felt as liquid vitality rather than mechanical jitter?

### 9. Scale & Peripheral Recognition
- [ ] **Small-Size Clarity**: Does the orb retain its distinct identity and core glow at 48px and 24px sizes?
- [ ] **Peripheral Presence**: Does the orb remain calm and non-distracting when viewed in peripheral vision?

---

## Acceptance Criterion
> Every item on this checklist must be marked **PASS** during a formal Visual Review in Material Studio before Sprint B (Industrial Design Freeze) is signed off.
