# Material Language

Constitution of physical optical material principles for Luca's embodiments.

---

## Foundational Principles

1. **The Orb is Never Metallic**
   - The body is made of thick optical glass and liquid. It has zero metallic sheen or mirror-like reflection.

2. **The Orb is Never Opaque**
   - Light always penetrates into the volume. Center transparency is preserved so the interior glows from within.

3. **The Orb is Never Perfectly Spherical**
   - Natural liquid surface tension creates subtle asymmetry and bottom teardrop gravitational sag. Perfect geometry feels artificial.

4. **The Orb Never Has Hard Phong Reflections**
   - Highlights are painterly, soft, and dual-layered (`coreSpot + softHalo`). Hard circular computer graphics reflections destroy the illusion.

5. **Refraction Dominates Over Reflection**
   - Optical refraction of background light through the volume gives the orb its tangible physical weight.

6. **Subsurface Light Suspension**
   - Core luminosity appears suspended deep inside the liquid volume, never painted on the outer boundary.
